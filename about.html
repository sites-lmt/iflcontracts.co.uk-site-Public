About IFL Contracts

Many years of experience, a genuinely personal service, and a simple mission: to connect good people with good work — and make getting paid effortless.

Who We Are

An independent UK recruitment agency & umbrella company.

IFL Contracts has many years' experience across UK recruitment and contractor payroll, building a trusted reputation on personal service and doing the simple things well.

As a recruitment agency, we place contract and permanent staff across construction, engineering, industrial, healthcare, IT and commercial sectors. As an umbrella company, we handle compliant payroll for contractors — so many of the people we place can be paid seamlessly through us from day one.

We work with agencies and employers throughout the UK, and we're familiar with their preferred working practices, ensuring a smooth experience and swift payment of all accounts.
