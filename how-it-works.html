How IFL Contracts Works

Being paid through an umbrella company should be simple. Here's exactly what happens when you contract through IFL — and everything we take care of on your behalf.

The Basics

What is an umbrella company?

An umbrella company acts as your employer while you carry out contract work through recruitment agencies or clients.

Instead of running your own limited company or being paid directly, you're employed by IFL Contracts. Your agency or client pays us for the work you complete, and we pay you a salary through PAYE — with all tax, National Insurance and deductions handled correctly.

It's the simplest, most compliant way to contract: no company accounts to file, no VAT returns to manage, and continuous employment across all of your assignments.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I join mid-contract?

Yes. You can join IFL Contracts at any time — pre or mid-contract. Just let us know the details of your assignment and we'll take care of the rest.

Am I tied in? Can I leave whenever I want?

There are no strings attached. You can leave whenever you wish. We only ask that any outstanding pay runs are completed so you receive everything you're owed.

How much does it cost?

Our fees are currently £20 per week or £75 per month, regardless of your income. There are no hidden or additional costs. See our Fees page for full details.

How quickly will I be paid?

We process payments immediately once cleared funds are received from your agency or client, and pay you with a clear, itemised payslip.

Do you work with my agency?

We work with all the major agencies throughout the UK and are familiar with their preferred working practices, ensuring swift payment of all accounts. If you're unsure, just ask us.

Is IFL Contracts HMRC compliant?

Absolutely. We operate full PAYE and follow all relevant HMRC and employment regulations, so you can contract with complete peace of mind.
