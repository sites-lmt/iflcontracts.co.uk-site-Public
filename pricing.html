Fee Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Does the fee change with how much I earn?

No. Our fee is a flat £20 per week or £75 per month regardless of your income. Unlike percentage-based providers, your fee never rises just because you earn more.

Are there any joining or leaving fees?

No. There are no set-up fees and no exit fees. You can join and leave whenever you wish, with no strings attached.

Is insurance really included?

Yes. Professional indemnity, employers' liability and public liability cover are all included as standard. If you need additional protection, we can arrange it to suit your role.

When is the fee taken?

The fee is deducted as part of each pay run, and shown clearly on your itemised payslip so you always know exactly what you've paid.
