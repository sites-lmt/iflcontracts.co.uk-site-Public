Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

IFL Contracts ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose your personal data and your rights in relation to that data, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018.

At a glance: We are the data controller for the personal data we hold about you. We collect data to help candidates find work, to help employers hire, to employ contractors and run payroll, and to meet our legal obligations. We never sell your data. You have rights, and you can contact us at any time.

2. The Data We Collect

2.1 Candidates & job applicants

  • Identity data: name, title and date of birth
  • Contact data: address, email address and telephone numbers
  • Application data: CV, work history, qualifications, references and right-to-work documentation
  • Placement data: roles applied for, interviews, availability and preferences

2.2 Contractors & employees

  • Identity data: name, title, date of birth, National Insurance number
  • Contact data: address, email address and telephone numbers
  • Right-to-work data: passport, visa or other documentation as required by law
  • Financial data: bank details, tax code, pay and deduction records
  • Contract data: assignment details, agency, rates, timesheets and payments

2.3 Employers & clients

  • Business contact details and details of the roles or staff you require

2.4 Enquiries & website visitors

  • Information you provide by email or phone
  • Limited technical data such as browser type and pages visited

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Match candidates with suitable roles and introduce them to employers
  • Employ contractors and administer their contracts of employment
  • Operate payroll, PAYE, National Insurance and pension arrangements
  • Invoice agencies and clients and manage credit control
  • Arrange insurance and indemnity cover
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide advice
  • Comply with our legal, tax and regulatory obligations (including HMRC)

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

We rely on one or more of the following lawful bases: performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests in running our business, and, where required, your consent.

5. Sharing Your Data

We may share your data with employers and clients (where you are a candidate), recruitment agencies and clients you work with, HMRC and other statutory bodies, pension and insurance providers, and trusted service providers who support our operations. We never sell your personal data.

6. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for, including satisfying any legal, accounting or reporting requirements. Payroll and tax records are typically retained for at least six years.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for the site to function. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect how the site works.

9. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
