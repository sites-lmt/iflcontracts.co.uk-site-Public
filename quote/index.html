Questions contractors ask

What does it cost?

One fee, agreed before you commit and confirmed in writing. It is deducted from the assignment value before employer costs and your payroll, and everything is included — invoicing, credit control, payroll, payslips and insurance. Nothing is billed on top.

How quickly will I be paid?

Once your approved timesheet is in and funds have cleared from your agency, payroll runs. Same-day payment options are available — ask when you sign up.

Can I switch umbrella company mid-contract?

Yes. Nothing stops you changing provider part-way through an assignment. You tell your agency where to send payment, we handle the onboarding, and there is no gap in your pay.

Does using an umbrella affect my IR35 position?

No. Your status is decided by the end client and the working practices of the assignment. Because you are paid through PAYE as an employee, the disguised remuneration exposure IR35 targets does not arise. Read more on our umbrella page.

Do I need to invoice anyone myself?

No. We raise the invoices on your agency, allocate the payments when they arrive, and chase anything overdue. Submitting a timesheet is the extent of your admin.
